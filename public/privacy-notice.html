Version 1.1 · Last updated 30 October 2026 · Applies to the website cyassure.eu

In short. We collect personal data on this website only when you fill in one of our forms. We use it to give you the download you asked for and to answer your message. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we never sell your data. You can withdraw your consent and use your rights at any time with the request form below or by email to privacy@cyassure.eu.

3. Who processes your data for us

We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone for their own purposes. These providers process it on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, the database that holds download registrations, and website security.
  • Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft 365): email, including confirmation emails and the mailboxes that receive your messages.
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts): web fonts (see above).

These providers run data centres in several countries, so your data may be processed outside India and outside the European Economic Area. Transfers out of India follow section 16 of the DPDP Act and any restriction the Government of India notifies. Transfers out of the EEA rely on an adequacy decision (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Withdraw your consent at any time (s.6(4)). It is as easy as giving it: use the request form below, or email us. We then stop processing your data, delete it, and keep only what DPDP Rule 8(3) or another law requires us to keep, for that purpose only. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Access information (s.11): a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction and erasure (s.12): to correct, complete or update your data, or have it erased.
  • Grievance redressal (s.13): to raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 5).
  • Nominate another person (s.14) to use these rights for you if you die or can no longer use them yourself.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the GDPR also gives you the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority (see section 6).

How to identify yourself. To exercise a right, send the request from, or give us, the email address you used on our forms. That is the identifier we hold for you (DPDP Rule 14(1)(b)). We may ask you to confirm it before we act.

India: if you are not satisfied with how our Grievance Officer handled your grievance, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, which works as a digital office, after first using our grievance process (DPDP Act s.13(3)).

European Economic Area: you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in your own country.

7. Children

This website and Cy360 are meant for businesses and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18, and our forms are for adults only. If we learn that a child under 18 has given us personal data without verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, we delete it. We never track, monitor the behaviour of, or target advertising at children (DPDP Act s.9).

8. Security and breaches

The website is served only over HTTPS. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers, access is limited to staff who need it, and access is logged. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay, together with the Data Protection Board of India (DPDP Rule 7) and, where the GDPR applies, the supervisory authority.
